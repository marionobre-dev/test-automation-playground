Dynamic content & waits

Each exercise needs a different waiting strategy. If you reach for a fixed sleep (waitForTimeout), your test will either be slow or flaky. Wait for the state you need instead.

1. Delayed element

Beginner

Click Start. A spinner shows for a random 2–5 seconds, then a heading appears.

Loading…

Hello World!

Expected: the spinner appears, then the finish heading becomes visible with the greeting and the spinner is gone. Your test must pass whether the load takes 2 or 5 seconds.

Show me how

2. Hidden vs removed

Beginner

One paragraph is hidden with CSS (still in the page), the other is removed from the DOM completely.

I am sometimes hidden.

I am sometimes removed.

Expected: after toggling, hidden-target is not visible but still exists (count 1), while removed-target doesn't exist at all (count 0). Toggling again brings both back.

Show me how

3. Enable / disable

Beginner

The input starts disabled. Clicking the button "calls a server" for 2 seconds, then enables it.

Expected: the input is disabled at first, enabled after the click (message It's enabled!), and you can type into it.

Show me how

4. Add / remove elements

Beginner

Beginner
